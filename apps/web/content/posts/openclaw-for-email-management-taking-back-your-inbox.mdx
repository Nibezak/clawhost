## OpenClaw for Email: Reclaim Your Inbox

Email is the black hole of productivity. OpenClaw changes that with intelligent, autonomous email management.

### The Problem

Modern email has volume, manual triage, context switching, repetition, and constant decisions.

### The Solution

An OpenClaw agent can:
- Intelligently filter and sort
- Draft and send autonomous responses
- Extract important data and deadlines
- Route emails to appropriate people
- Generate daily digests
- Integrate with calendar and tasks

### The Payoff

Organizations report 40-50% reduction in email management time, fewer missed items, better organization, and reduced stress.
