# Why You Should Self-Host Your VPN

Commercial VPN providers promise privacy and security, but there's a fundamental problem: you're trusting a third party with all your traffic. Self-hosting eliminates that trust requirement entirely.

## The Problem with Commercial VPNs

When you use a commercial VPN service, all your internet traffic flows through their servers. You have to trust that they:

- Don't log your activity (despite their claims)
- Properly secure their infrastructure
- Won't hand over data under legal pressure
- Aren't selling your browsing patterns to advertisers

Multiple commercial VPN providers have been caught violating their own no-log policies. When privacy is the product, trust shouldn't be a requirement.

## The Self-Hosted Advantage

With a self-hosted VPN like OpenClaw on ClawHost, you get:

### True Privacy

Your traffic flows through a server that only you control. There are no third parties, no shared infrastructure, and no one else can access your data.

### Dedicated Performance

Commercial VPNs share servers among thousands of users. Your self-hosted instance has dedicated resources — no throttling, no congestion, full bandwidth.

### Complete Control

You have root SSH access to your server. Install additional tools, customize configurations, or use the server for other purposes alongside your VPN.

### Transparent Costs

No misleading "lifetime deals" or hidden fees. You pay a flat monthly rate for your server's resources. Cancel anytime.

Self-hosting your VPN isn't just about privacy — it's about taking control of your digital infrastructure.
