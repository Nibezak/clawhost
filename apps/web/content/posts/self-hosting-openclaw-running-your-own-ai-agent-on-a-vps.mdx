## Self-Hosting OpenClaw: Your Own AI Infrastructure

One of OpenClaw's key advantages: run it entirely on your own servers. No cloud dependency, no external service, complete control.

### Why Self-Host?

- **Data Privacy** - your data never leaves your servers
- **Cost Efficiency** - no per-API-call charges
- **Customization** - modify for your needs
- **Reliability** - control your own uptime
- **Security** - firewall behind your infrastructure

### Basic Setup

- Get a VPS (2 CPU, 4GB RAM minimum)
- Install Node.js and Docker
- Clone OpenClaw
- Configure environment
- Set up database
- Create your first agent

### The Self-Hosting Advantage

Complete data control, cost savings at scale, faster responses, freedom to customize, compliance with regulations.
