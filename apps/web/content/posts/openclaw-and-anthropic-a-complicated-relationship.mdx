## OpenClaw and Anthropic: A Nuanced Relationship

The relationship between OpenClaw and Anthropic (Claude's creator) is frequently misunderstood.

### What's True

OpenClaw can integrate with Claude, and many users choose it. Both projects share values around AI safety and transparency.

### What's Not True

OpenClaw is **not** owned by Anthropic. It's an independent open-source project that can work with Claude, GPT, or other models.

### The Reality

OpenClaw and Anthropic have a **partnership of choice, not ownership** — separate projects that work well together.
