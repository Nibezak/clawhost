# ClawHost vs Commercial VPNs

When it comes to choosing a VPN solution, you have two fundamentally different approaches: commercial VPN services that share infrastructure among millions of users, or self-hosted solutions where you own the server entirely.

## Privacy

**Commercial VPNs** claim no-log policies, but you're trusting their word. Several providers have been caught logging user data despite promises otherwise. Your traffic passes through their infrastructure, and you have zero visibility into what happens to it.

**ClawHost** gives you a dedicated server that only you control. No shared infrastructure, no third-party access, no trust required. You can verify everything yourself via SSH.

## Performance

**Commercial VPNs** share servers among thousands of concurrent users. During peak hours, speeds drop significantly. Many providers throttle bandwidth on cheaper plans.

**ClawHost** provides dedicated resources. Your CPU, RAM, and bandwidth are yours alone. No throttling, no congestion, consistent performance 24/7.

## Cost

**Commercial VPNs** typically charge $3-12/month, often requiring annual commitments for the best rates. The cheapest plans come with significant limitations.

**ClawHost** starts at $4.50/month with no commitment. Monthly billing, cancel anytime. You know exactly what server resources you're getting.

## Control

**Commercial VPNs** offer a one-size-fits-all app. You can't customize the server, install additional tools, or use the infrastructure for anything else.

**ClawHost** gives you full root SSH access. Install monitoring tools, run additional services, customize your VPN configuration — it's your server.

## Setup

**Commercial VPNs** are plug-and-play: download the app, log in, connect.

**ClawHost** is nearly as simple: sign up, click deploy, import your WireGuard config. The entire process takes under 60 seconds with zero technical knowledge required.

## The Verdict

If you value true privacy, dedicated performance, and full control over your infrastructure, self-hosting with ClawHost is the clear choice. The setup is just as easy, the cost is comparable, and you never have to trust a third party with your data.
