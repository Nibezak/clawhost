## Cron Jobs: Making Your Agent Work on a Schedule

One of OpenClaw's superpowers is scheduling agents to run automatically. Define what, when, and how — the agent handles execution.

### Why Schedule?

- **Background work** - research and reports while you focus
- **Consistency** - same reliable process every time
- **Efficiency** - off-peak processing saves resources
- **Reporting** - daily digests, weekly summaries

### Practical Examples

- Daily email digest at 8 AM
- Nightly backups at 2 AM
- Weekly report generation Friday at 5 PM
- Hourly content publishing
- Monthly billing automation

### The Power

Properly scheduled agents automate what would otherwise be manual work, enabling organizations to scale without adding headcount.
