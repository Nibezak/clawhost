## Voice Interaction: Speaking with Your OpenClaw Agent

Text isn't always best. Sometimes you want to talk to your agent naturally. OpenClaw's voice capabilities make this possible.

### How It Works

- Speech to text (transcription) converts your speech
- Agent thinks and decides
- Text to speech (synthesis) responds in natural language
- Full duplex allows interruption and natural flow

### Practical Applications

- Personal assistant - wake up and ask about your day
- Hands-free operation - while driving, cooking, working
- Remote support - troubleshooting naturally
- Accessibility - voice-only interface
- Education - interactive learning
