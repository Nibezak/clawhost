## The Origin Story: How a Vacation Idea Became OpenClaw

Peter Steinberger was on vacation when he started thinking about AI agents. He noticed a gap: most AI tools were too passive or too limited. What if you could build an AI that actually _does_ things?

### The Early Days

The idea started as "Clawdbot" — a playful reference to a digital "hand" reaching out. As the project grew, trademark issues led to pivoting to "Moltbot," then finally "OpenClaw."

### The Explosion

OpenClaw exploded in popularity because it solved a real problem: agents that actually work, are open-source, and are practical for real use today.

### ClawHost

While OpenClaw remained open-source, ClawHost emerged as a commercial platform providing managed hosting and professional support for organizations wanting ready-to-deploy agents.
