# Getting Started with OpenClaw

OpenClaw makes it simple to deploy your own VPN server on dedicated infrastructure. In this guide, we'll walk you through deploying your first instance with ClawHost.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you'll need:

- A ClawHost account (sign up takes 30 seconds)
- A device to connect from (any OS works)
- A WireGuard client installed on your device

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Head over to ClawHost and sign in with your email. We use passwordless authentication — just enter your email and click the magic link we send you.

## Step 2: Deploy Your First Claw

Once logged in, click **Deploy New** from your dashboard. You'll be asked to:

1. **Name your Claw** — give it a memorable name like "my-first-claw"
2. **Choose a location** — pick the server location closest to you for best performance
3. **Select a plan** — start with the smallest plan if you're just testing

Click deploy, and your OpenClaw instance will be live in under 60 seconds.

## Step 3: Connect

After deployment, you'll receive your connection details. Import the WireGuard configuration into your client and connect. That's it — you're now routing through your own dedicated VPN server.
